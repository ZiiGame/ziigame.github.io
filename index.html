My Story

Like many players, my journey started with classic games. From NHL '97 on the Sega Genesis, DOS games at school, Wolfenstein 3D, Age of Empires, Need for Speed II, Red Alert 2, StarCraft, Warcraft and World of Warcraft. Those games shaped the way I think about game design.

Today many AAA games look incredible, but often miss the gameplay depth that made older games unforgettable. I believe fun always comes before graphics.

That's why I decided to create my own games and share every step of the journey through YouTube development logs.

My childhood dream was to create a game studio. Today that dream has a name: Zii Game.
