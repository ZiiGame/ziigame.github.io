My Story

Like many players, my journey started with classic games. From NHL '97 on the Sega Genesis, DOS games at school, Wolfenstein 3D, Age of Empires and Need for Speed II, to unforgettable hours playing Red Alert 2, StarCraft, Warcraft and later World of Warcraft. Those games shaped the way I think about game design.

Over the years I felt that many AAA games became visually incredible, but often lost the gameplay depth that made older titles so memorable. Indie games reminded me that creativity and fun always matter more than massive budgets.

That's why I decided to build my own games and share the entire journey openly. Every milestone, every mistake and every improvement will be documented through development logs on YouTube.

My childhood dream was simple: one day create my own game studio. Today that dream has a name — Zii Game. Thank you for being part of this adventure.
